# 🌴 HH Goa 2026 — Builder ID Card Generator

> Turn your photo into your HH Goa 2026 Builder Card and share your builder identity with the world. 🚀

A fast, mobile-friendly web application that allows users to upload a photo, enter their name and role/stack, and instantly generate a branded **HH Goa 2026 Builder Card**.

The generated card can be downloaded as an image and shared on X with **#FrameInGoa**.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📸 Upload your profile photo
- 🖼️ Supports JPG, PNG and HEIC images
- 🎨 Automatically generates a branded HH Goa 2026 card
- 👤 Add your name and role/stack
- ⚡ Fast image generation
- 👀 Live card preview
- 📥 Download the final card as PNG
- 🐦 Share your card on X
- #️⃣ Automatically includes `#FrameInGoa`
- 📱 Fully responsive and mobile-friendly
- 🚫 No login or signup required
